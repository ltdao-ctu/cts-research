# Machine learning-based approach for disease severity classification of carpal tunnel syndrome

## One-line thesis
Across 1,037 CTS hands (507 mild, 276 moderate, 254 severe) using 11 clinical and ultrasound variables (CSA and Palmar Bowing), 3-class XGBoost reaches 76.6% test accuracy (Moderate balanced acc 71.8% vs Mild 83.6% and Severe 83.5%), while OvR stacked ensemble + SMOTE reaches 90.9% accuracy (0.95 ROC) for Severe-vs-Rest and shows Palmar Bowing ranks higher in importance than CSA.
