# Application of Deep Learning Algorithms in Automatic Sonographic Localization and Segmentation of the Median Nerve: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

## One-line thesis
Meta-analysis confirming U-Net, Mask R-CNN, and DeepLab achieve high pooled Dice (~0.88-0.91) and precision/recall for automated median nerve segmentation on carpal tunnel ultrasound.
