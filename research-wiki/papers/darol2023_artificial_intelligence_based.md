# Artificial Intelligence Based Rating of Carpal Tunnel Syndrome Efficacy in Clinical Diagnosis

## One-line thesis
SVM achieves 93.55% accuracy (0.94 AUC) for 4-class Padua CTS severity grading on 315 patients using 17 demographic and Median/Ulnar NCS parameters, outperforming Ensemble (80.65%), Decision Tree (70.97%), and KNN (64.52%), though full-text audit reveals circular NCS-to-Padua label leakage and only 11 severe cases.
