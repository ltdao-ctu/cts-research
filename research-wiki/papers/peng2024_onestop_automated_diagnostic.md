# One-Stop Automated Diagnostic System for Carpal Tunnel Syndrome in Ultrasound Images Using Deep Learning

## One-line thesis
Full-text audit of OSA-CTSD on 32,301 wrist ultrasound sweep frames (130 videos, 81 subjects): SegFormer-B2 (24.73M params, 47.28 FPS) achieves 85.78% Dice and 7.21 px HD95 (frame CSA MAE 1.71 mm²), and an explainable Random Forest classifier achieves 93.85% accuracy and 0.98 AUC (beating inexperienced radiologists at 90.26% and matching experienced radiologists at 98.21%, p=0.08), with Swelling Ratio (SR) contributing 42.9% feature importance (0.96 AUC) vs only 0.6% (0.57 AUC) for Max Flattening Ratio.
