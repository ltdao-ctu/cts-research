# MorphoTex-CTS: Disentangled Morphology-vs-Echotexture Network with Intra-Patient Comparative Normalization

**stage:** `proposed`  ·  **outcome:** `pending`

## Thesis
Orthogonalizing median nerve silhouette geometry and reference-normalized intraneural echotexture reveals stage-specific sensitivity across mild vs. severe CTS and outperforms CSA-only and unmasked CNN baselines.

## Key risks
Masking nerve boundaries could omit perineurial context; mitigated via dilated epineurial ring ablation.
