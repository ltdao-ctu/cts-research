# EDX2US-KD: Cross-Modal Knowledge Distillation from Raw Nerve Conduction Waveforms to Point-of-Care B-Mode Ultrasound

**stage:** `proposed`  ·  **outcome:** `pending`

## Thesis
Distilling continuous electrophysiological representations from a privileged NCS-waveform teacher into an ultrasound-only student improves 4-grade CTS severity classification without requiring EDX at test time.

## Key risks
Small paired US+EDX sample size; mitigated via relational distance distillation and monotonic regularization.
